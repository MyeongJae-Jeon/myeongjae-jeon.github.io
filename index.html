About

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Dori Bejleri. I received my master’s degree from Seoul National University, where I was advised by Donghoon Hyeon.

I am on the academic job market for the 2026–27 cycle. View my CV.

Research interests

I study algebraic geometry, especially the birational geometry of algebraic stacks and its interplay with moduli theory. I am also interested in connections between algebraic geometry and computer science, particularly autoformalization and computer vision.

Publications and preprints

Pure Mathematics

  • Resolution of indeterminacy of rational maps to proper tame stacks

    Preprint, 2025

    arXiv:2506.14969

    Summary

    We resolve the indeterminacy of rational maps from regular surfaces to tame stacks over excellent bases. In characteristic zero, we treat regular source schemes of arbitrary dimension and prove a purity lemma for tame stacks.
